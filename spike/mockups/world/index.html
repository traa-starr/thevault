The Vault: one world

A tap-through of the redesigned app, with made-up songs and lines. It is only a picture of where every control lives.

Judge the moves below on your phone. Nothing here saves, plays or talks to the laptop.

Open the app

What moved where

Home

  • Gear is now in the top-right ... menu, with Sort and Tag songs.
  • The search bar is gone; the round Search button is its only home.
  • Favorites shuffle: hold Shuffle, pick Mine, Favorites or Unsorted.
  • Two rows left Home (Most played, Versions to compare); both live in Library.

Library

  • Shuffle and Order moved into the header ....
  • Hold a row opens the song sheet (it used to start Select; Select is a row in it).
  • The Versions chip on a row is gone: its second line says "3 versions".

Player

  • The eye is gone. Mine and Not mine show only while a song is unsorted.
  • Archive, Queue, Versions and Add as a version live in the ... song sheet.
  • Queue: swipe up on the art. Shuffle moved into the Queue header.
  • The tap on the time-left is gone; it always shows the length.

Sort

  • Tap a set to see its cards (it used to open and close).
  • All mine and All not mine: hold a set.

Cards

  • Add as a version: hold the card (the + Version button is gone).

Versions

  • Tap a version to play it. Its menu (Make main, Remove, Not mine) is on hold.

Add it to the Home Screen

1. Open this page in Safari.

2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

3. Open it from the new icon, so it runs full screen.

Haptics, sound and real glass can't show in a web page. Buttons are solid stand-ins, and the buzz on a tap only exists in the real app.
