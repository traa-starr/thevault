The Vault, three ways

Same songs, same tabs. The difference is what you see when you open the app at night. All songs and lines are made up.

WallA song to hear. Colour tiles everywhere, like Apple Music; the player is the tile. LineA box to search and your own lines. "Rap a line" first, waveform with tag dots, almost no art. DealA card to judge. One forgotten song at a time, swipe to Fire or Shelve. For each one: open it, then Share, Add to Home Screen, and open it from there (Safari's bars cover the bottom otherwise). Haptics, sound, real glass and speed can't show in a web page.
